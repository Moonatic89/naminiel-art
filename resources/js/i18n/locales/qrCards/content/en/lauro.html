In the Silence of the Forest

The sun sank slowly beyond the treetops, brushing the rain-bright leaves with gold and rose. Beneath Tempora's castle, the forest held a rarefied quiet, broken only by the stream murmuring under the old stone bridge.

Lauro sat on one of the arches, relaxed in posture and distant in thought, while Selvanello hopped around him, turning anything within reach into a toy.

"Hey, sire!" he called, waving a root like a sceptre. "What are you staring at? A magic fish? A treasure? Or are you thinking about that thing you saw again?"

Lauro lifted his eyes with the faintest smile. "Do not call me sire, Selvanello. I told you already: I am not, and never will be."

"Oh, but you are more than a king to me, Lauro!" Selvanello replied, bowing so clumsily he nearly toppled over. "But you still have not answered. That creature... what was it? A deer? A shadow? A fairy?"

Lauro tilted his head, as if searching for words among the ripples. "I do not know," he murmured. "I do not even know whether she was real."

He had seen her, yes: gentle, quiet, as if the weight of the world had vanished for one breath. And yet something in her felt wrong, like a silence that did not merely surround things, but filled them.

Selvanello blinked. "I understand none of this, Lauro. But silence can be good, right?"

Lauro listened to the stream answer in his place. "Perhaps. But I cannot forget her. And I do not know whether that is a gift... or a sentence."

Selvanello sprang upright, brandishing his root with all the drama of a young knight. "Whatever she is, sire, if she returns, we will face her together! You and me! Two heroes of the forest!"

Lauro looked at him, and a tired, genuine smile softened his face. "Yes, Selvanello. Together."
