Toward the World to Change

The clearing stretched quietly beneath the golden twilight. Dry leaves crackled under Friday's steps as she bounced forward, every movement bright with unstoppable energy.

Behind her, Kumirai advanced with slow, solemn steps. The bear's coat was blue as night, and though he did not speak, his deep eyes followed every leap of his young companion.

"So, so! Here we are, Kumirai! We are officially on a journey! A journey to change the world. No small thing!"

The bear kept walking. Friday did not seem to need an answer. She stopped with her hands on her hips and gave him a crafty smile.

"But what if changing the world means giving it a cape? No, wait, a giant hat! Or maybe we flip it over, like shaking apples out of a tree!"

She burst out laughing, raising her arms as if overturning an imaginary globe. Kumirai watched her with solemn patience and the faintest suggestion of amusement.

"Wouldn't it be amazing if the world had a cloak of stars? We enter the scene, me with my fists, you with your huge paws, BAM! Everything gets better. Epic, right?"

She walked backward, grinning. "Anyway, it feels so good to have run away. Tempora always there, with that serious face, saying: do this, do that, be responsible. Being responsible is more exhausting than running!"

For a moment Friday puffed out her chest and swore, with ridiculous ceremony, that she would protect the world with all her courage. The pose lasted only a breath before laughter took her again.

Kumirai lifted her gently with one enormous paw, setting her back on her feet.

"Thanks, friend. I knew you were the best travel companion. And you know what? However long the road is, I cannot wait to discover everything."

Her voice softened. "And... someone is waiting for me. I do not know who or where, but I feel it. That is the most exciting part."

Kumirai's silence wrapped around her like a promise: whatever waited ahead, he would stay by her side.
