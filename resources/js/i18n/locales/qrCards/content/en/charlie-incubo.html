The Red Thread of Fate

Once, in the hidden realm of forgotten weavings, people said that a red thread bound the destiny of two souls, interlaced forever between light and shadow.

Their names were Charlie and Incubo: two faces of the same coin, two essences born from the same breath of the world, and yet divided by an eternal fracture.

Charlie walked among ruins of a lost age, wrapped in clothes that seemed to carry dawn across his body. In his eyes lived the first light of day, a promise of hope that was never quite enough to dissolve the darkness following him.

The red thread slipped through his fingers, drawing him toward something, or someone, he could not ignore. Every step and every memory gathered from the ruins brought him closer to a shadow he could not banish.

At the other end stood Incubo, distant and yet terribly near, a melancholy reflection in the world's half-light. His pale face carried broken dreams and quiet nights. There was no cruelty in him, only a sadness that seemed to rise from the soul itself.

They walked opposite paths, pulled toward each other and separated only by the thin border between light and shadow. The thread stretched, never breaking, holding their worlds in a delicate balance.

Their bond was not made of words, but of interwoven silences and memories returning like reflections on water. They were opposites, and still inseparable.

At the heart of that red thread, where fate had tied them together, there was a promise: never to abandon each other, not even when time itself tried to divide them.
